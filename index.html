HOW TO PLAY

  • Earn birds: Choose the missing word. Every correct answer gives you one Red Bird.
  • Three chances: Three wrong answers end the level. Restarting restores all three chances.
  • Streak bonus: Every four correct answers in a row earns a random special bird.
  • Aim and launch: Drag the bird back on the slingshot, then release it. Birds can bounce into several targets.
  • Use abilities: Tap while a bird is flying. Yellow dashes, Blue splits, Black explodes, and White drops an egg.
  • Move the camera: Drag empty space to pan, pinch to zoom, or double-click to reset the view.
  • Win the level: Defeat all 15 pigs. If you run out of birds, answer another question to earn more.
  • Use chain reactions: Fast, heavy falling blocks deal more damage. Stone hits hardest, while ice breaks more easily.
  • Watch for helmets: Each level has one helmet pig with twice the health of a normal pig.
